The Chemico Group · Chemico Onsite prototype

One portal, eight screens, two loops

Material and chemistry data arrives from the plant floor and from Sage, lands in eight role-scoped screens, and leaves as an action — a request, a dispatch, a delivery. Nothing here shows pricing.

Access layer
One super admin sets sites × modules for every person. Role templates give the defaults:
Site buyer · 3 modules Crib attendant · 2 Fluids & quality · 3 Plant admin · 7 Chemico rep · 5, all sites

Chemico rep · Zebra TC22
Scan-in of delivered stock, and the chemistry round walked system by system.

What the plant gets
Stock held between its minimum and maximum — no line stop, no over-holding.
Every order traceable to the person who asked and the trigger that raised it.
Coolant and washers inside their control band, caught in the round that drifts.
One audit trail: scan, request, order, delivery, scan-in.
Evidence for the quarterly review, without anyone building a deck.
